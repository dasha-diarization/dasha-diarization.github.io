The paper

DASHA: Diarization-Aware Speech-LLM ASR for Healthcare in Code-Switched Indic Doctor-Patient Conversations

Community primary care in rural India is recorded in the field with one distant microphone. Turns are short, they overlap, and speakers mix Hindi and English. Extracting what the patient reports is then a speaker-attributed ASR problem, not a single-talker transcription problem.

The paper studies this on DISPLACE-M: 125 development dialogues (24.5 hours, 30.1k turns) and a blind EVAL1 set (10.1 hours). Overlap is only 4.35% of the audio, but it sits on frequent turn changes. The official metrics are DER with overlap and no collar, tcpWER for SA-ASR, and ROUGE F1 for condition lists.

The cascade

Diarization, then SA-ASR, then extraction.

Each module can be swapped on its own. That is how the paper shows that a better diarizer helps extraction only when the ASR is the adapted one.

01 · D3

EEND-VC diarization

w2v-BERT 2.0 with LoRA, an LSTM context network, a powerset classifier, and ResNet-34 embeddings clustered with k-means (k=2). Best EVAL1 DER: 7.76% after in-domain fine-tuning on C1.

02 · A4 / A5

Speaker-attributed ASR

Qwen3-ASR-1.7B transcribes diarized turns only. Fine-tuning on public Hindi then DISPLACE-M, plus Unicode normalization, reaches 19.27% tcpWER. Optional dialogue-level GEC reaches 18.59%.

03 · LLM

Condition extraction

An LLM reads the Hinglish SA-ASR transcript (cascade) or the audio directly (E2E). Best open cascade: Gemma 3 12B, 28.97 ROUGE-1. Gemini 3 Pro on audio is the empirical ceiling at 45.60.

EVAL1

In-domain data moved the needle more than architecture.

Fine-tuning on DISPLACE-M cut 0.8–1.1 DER points and 6.1 tcpWER points. A larger meeting-style pre-training set, a Mamba context network, and a 2-speaker output each changed DER by at most 0.44 points.

On code-switched words, the multilingual encoder (D1 vs DiariZen) reduces speaker confusion by 45% relative. Generic Hindi fine-tuning alone can raise CS-WER; in-domain Hinglish recovers those words. The paper therefore reports CS-DER and CS-WER next to the usual averages.

A module-swap with Gemma 3 12B is the practical takeaway: upgrading only ASR helps extraction (25.16 → 27.90 ROUGE-1); upgrading only diarization can hurt (23.72); upgrading both is best (28.97).
